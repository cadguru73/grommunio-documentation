### Upstream projects

The reference sections are kept in sync with the documentation shipped by the
[Gromox](https://github.com/grommunio/gromox) project (man pages, protocol
specifications, wiki) and the
[grommunio admin-api](https://github.com/grommunio/admin-api) project (command
reference and OpenAPI specification). The *Project setup* pages reproduce the
READMEs of those two projects and of
[grommunio-web](https://github.com/grommunio/grommunio-web),
[grommunio-sync](https://github.com/grommunio/grommunio-sync),
[grommunio-dav](https://github.com/grommunio/grommunio-dav),
[admin-web](https://github.com/grommunio/admin-web) and
[grommunio-index](https://github.com/grommunio/grommunio-index). The many
contributors to those projects are credited in the respective repositories.

The *IMAP migration with imapsync* page describes
[imapsync](https://imapsync.lamiral.info/) by Gilles Lamiral, which grommunio
ships as the `imapsync` package; its option and exit-code tables summarise the
imapsync README, which, like the tool itself, is published under the
[No Limit Public License (NLPL)](https://imapsync.lamiral.info/LICENSE).
